Privacy notice

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This notice explains what personal data is involved when you use Tolga Player and its website. The controller is Tolga Çiftciçelik, an individual based in Istanbul, Türkiye. Contact: cctolga@gmail.com.

The app

Tolga Player has no account and no analytics, and it does not send us any data. Your videos never leave your home: when you use AirPlay, they go directly from your Mac to your Apple TV over your local network.

  • On your Mac only: your settings, playlist, Apple TV pairing, license key and trial start date are stored in Tolga Player's folder on your Mac (~/Library/Application Support/Tolga).
  • Subtitle search: when you use "Search Online", Tolga Player sends the search to OpenSubtitles.com: a title taken from the file name, a fingerprint (hash) of the video file and your chosen languages, plus your OpenSubtitles user name and password if you choose to log in. OpenSubtitles handles this under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent if you don't use this feature.
  • License check: license keys are checked on your Mac, without contacting a server.

Buying a license

Purchases are handled by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle collects the details needed for the sale (such as your name, email address, country and payment details) and processes them under Paddle's privacy policy. Paddle shares with us your name, email address, country and order details so that we can send your license key and help you. We never see your full payment details.

We keep these order details for as long as the law requires (for tax and accounting) and to be able to re-send your license key, and use them only for that and to answer your emails.

This website

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web host, they receive your IP address and browser details when you visit, under their own privacy policies. The website sets no cookies and has no analytics.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email only to answer you.
