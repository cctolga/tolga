Questions

Which Apple TV do I need?

An Apple TV with tvOS 26 or later, on the same network as your Mac. Tolga finds it on its own.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 and later) and macOS 12 or later.

Does it lower the picture quality?

No. The picture is sent exactly as it is in the file, 4K and all. Only sound the Apple TV can't play, like DTS or TrueHD, is converted, to Dolby Digital Plus with the 5.1 channels kept.

Are my movies uploaded anywhere?

No. Tolga sends them straight from your Mac to your Apple TV over your home network. There is no account and no cloud.

What happens after the 14 days?

Tolga keeps playing everything on your Mac for free. Sending to the Apple TV asks for a license key, which you get by email when you buy.

Can I get my money back?

Yes, within 30 days of buying, no questions asked. Orders are handled by Paddle, our reseller; see the refund policy.

Is Tolga open source?

The player is, under the Apache License 2.0; its source code is on GitHub. AirPlay is a separate, closed-source part of the official app, and that is what a license unlocks.
